OUR PASSION & BACKGROUND

Custom 3D Printing & Scale Design Fabrication

Welcome to Rage3DesigNZ! We specialize in bringing unique, custom-made shapes, models, and intricate prototypes to life. Utilizing modern desktop manufacturing techniques, our goal is to design premium structural solutions and creative display designs. Whether you are looking for articulation mechanics like our popular snake and octopus figures or tracking down custom display layout objects, we forge ahead with extreme precision.

Our workspace focuses heavily on optimizing custom workflows, ensuring clean layering parameters, and implementing custom structural features for complex projects. Everything produced in our studio undergoes thorough safety and quality checks to ensure beautiful engineering definitions and structural balance from top to bottom.
